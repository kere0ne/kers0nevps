FAQ

What happens when my bot crashes?

The watchdog detects the exit and restarts the container automatically. If it crash-loops more than five times in ten minutes, it is stopped and flagged in the panel so you can fix the bug without burning restart cycles.

Where do my bot tokens live?

Server-side only. You set them as environment variables in the panel and they are injected into the container at runtime, stored encrypted on the host. They are never sent to the browser or embedded in your uploaded files.

Can I run anything besides Discord bots?

If it fits in a container and respects your resource limits, yes. Webhooks, APIs, cron jobs, all fair game.

How is this different from a normal VPS?

You get the same isolation and control without managing the OS, firewall, or process manager. The panel handles deployment, logs, and restarts so you only think about your code.
